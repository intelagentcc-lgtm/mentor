**Student**

Great to hear about your gardening and sketching experiences! For exploring landscape design, you could start by creating a small garden bed and documenting its layout and features in your sketchbook. This will help you understand how plants, colors, and textures interact in a space. After that, you could look up some basic landscape design principles online or in books to see how these elements are typically used. Finally, consider reaching out to a local landscape designer for a brief consultation to get some insights and ideas. Keep your sketchbook updated with your observations and designs, and you'll be well on your way to understanding landscape design principles.

### Follow-up 1 · chat

**Student**

How can I describe the place without using its real name?

**Mentor**

To describe a place without using its real name, you can use descriptive language to paint a picture in the reader's mind. For example, if you're describing a park, you might say it has 'lush green grass,' 'majestic oak trees,' and 'a babbling brook.' You can also describe the smells, sounds, and feelings associated with the place. This approach will help you keep the place anonymous while still making it vivid. You can also use metaphors or personification to describe the place. For instance, you could say the park is like a 'green oasis' or that the brook is 'singing a soothing melody.' This method will work well with your sketchbook and writing project.

## Session 5 · chat

**Student**

I have four kinds labeled and a key. How can I check that the path is easy to see before I hand it in?

**Mentor**

To check that the path is easy to see before you hand in your garden design, you can use a few strategies. First, ensure the path is clearly marked with a different color or material from the plants and other garden elements. You could use a contrasting color for the path or even a different type of mulch or gravel. Additionally, consider the contrast between the path and the surrounding plants. If the path is a lighter color, it might be easier to see against a darker background. Lastly, you could add some reflective elements to the path, like reflective tape or paint, to make it stand out even more. Make sure to include these details in your key and main path section of your design. [S1]
