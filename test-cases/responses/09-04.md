## Session 1 · explore

**Student**

What beginner activity could help me explore landscape design using my garden and sketchbook?

**Mentor**

Great to hear about your interests in landscape design! Using your garden and sketchbook, you can start by experimenting with different plant combinations and layouts. Try sketching out simple designs and then implementing them in your garden. For example, you could create a small section dedicated to a specific type of plant or a themed garden like a butterfly garden. This hands-on approach will help you understand how plants grow and interact in different spaces. Additionally, you might consider adding a few basic tools like a rake, trowel, and watering can to your garden toolkit. Keep a journal of your garden's progress and changes, which will be useful for your sketchbook. This project will not only enhance your landscape design skills but also give you a lot of creative freedom. Good luck with your garden and sketchbook explorations!

**Mentor**

To check that the path is easy to see before you hand it in, you should ensure that the path is clearly marked and distinct from the other elements of your garden design. Since you have a key, you can use it to verify that the path is correctly labeled and distinguishable. Make sure the path is not too similar in color or texture to the surrounding areas. If you have the key, you can compare the path's representation in the key with how it's drawn in your garden design. This will help you confirm that the path is clearly visible and easy to follow. If you need to make adjustments, you can use the key to guide your changes. Good job on including the key, it's a great tool for checking your work. Keep up the good work with your garden design!
