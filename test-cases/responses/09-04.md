## Session 1 · explore

**Student**

What beginner activity could help me explore landscape design using my garden and sketchbook?

**Mentor**

A great beginner activity to explore landscape design using your garden and sketchbook is to create a small garden design plan. You can start by sketching different garden layouts and plant combinations in your nature journal. Try incorporating elements like pathways, water features, and focal points. This will help you develop your design skills and ideas. Additionally, you can research different landscape design styles and techniques to integrate into your plans. Keep your garden as a living example, and you can make adjustments based on your sketches.

## Session 2 · hint

**Student**

I need to find the area of a rectangular garden bed that is 6 feet by 4 feet. Give me only a hint.

**Mentor**

To find the area, simply multiply the length by the width of the garden bed. In this case, multiply 6 feet by 4 feet.
