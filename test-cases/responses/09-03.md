## Session 1 · plan

**Student**

Help me make a weekly clarinet plan with three 15-minute practice sessions.

**Mentor**

Great! Let's break your weekly clarinet practice plan into manageable sessions. Here are two actions to start with:

**Action 1:** Plan your practice sessions for the week. Decide which days you can dedicate to clarinet practice and allocate 15 minutes each session.

**Action 2:** Write down what you want to practice each session. For example, focus on technique, reading music, or playing a specific piece.

**Action 3:** Set a goal for each practice session. Whether it's mastering a new scale or improving your timing, having a goal will help you stay focused and motivated.

Remember to include these sessions in your overall weekly schedule to ensure you're practicing regularly.

## Session 2 · checkin

**Student**

I played the first two days, but stopped when I reached a difficult rhythm. How should I adjust?

**Mentor**

It sounds like you're making good progress with your clarinet practice. Here’s a suggestion to adjust your approach for the difficult rhythm session:

**Action:** Try to break down the difficult rhythm into smaller, manageable parts. Start by counting each note slowly and accurately, then gradually increase the speed as you feel more comfortable. This can help you build confidence and improve your timing without feeling overwhelmed.

Remember to include this in your weekly plan and keep practicing. If you need any more tips, feel free to ask!

## Session 3 · example

**Student**

Show a worked example of how to count a measure with two quarter notes and four eighth notes in 4/4.

**Mentor**

Assuming the notes are in the order listed, start each note on these counts: 1, 2, 3, and after 3, 4, and after 4. A quarter note lasts one beat. An eighth note lasts half a beat. They add up to four beats. Tap a steady “1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and” and clap only on the listed counts.
