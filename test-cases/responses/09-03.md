## Session 3 · example

**Student**

Show a worked example of how to count a measure with two quarter notes and four eighth notes in 4/4.

**Mentor**

Assuming the notes are in the order listed, start each note on these counts: 1, 2, 3, and after 3, 4, and after 4. A quarter note lasts one beat. An eighth note lasts half a beat. They add up to four beats. Tap a steady “1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and” and clap only on the listed counts.
