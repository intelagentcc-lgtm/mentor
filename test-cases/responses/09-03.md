## Session 2 · checkin

**Student**

I played the first two days, but stopped when I reached a difficult rhythm. How should I adjust?

**Mentor**

It sounds like you're making good progress with your clarinet practice. For the session where you encountered the difficult rhythm, let's adjust your approach. Instead of stopping, try to count the rhythm slowly and break it down into smaller, manageable parts. You can also use a metronome to help you maintain a steady beat. Once you feel more comfortable with the rhythm, gradually increase the speed. This will help you build your confidence and improve your technique. How does this sound?

## Session 3 · example

**Student**

Show a worked example of how to count a measure with two quarter notes and four eighth notes in 4/4.

**Mentor**

Assuming the notes are in the order listed, start each note on these counts: 1, 2, 3, and after 3, 4, and after 4. A quarter note lasts one beat. An eighth note lasts half a beat. They add up to four beats. Tap a steady “1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and” and clap only on the listed counts.

Here’s a plan for your 15-minute practice session: Session 1: Start with a metronome to help you maintain a steady beat. Begin with a slow tempo and gradually increase the speed.
