## Session 1 · explore

**Student**

Suggest a small project to compare communication design and editing using my yearbook skills.

**Mentor**

Great work on your yearbook experience! For your small project comparing communication design and editing, you could create a mock-up of a cover page for a magazine or a brochure. Use your graphic design skills to design the layout and typography, then compare it with a real magazine cover or brochure. Analyze how the design elements like color, typography, and layout contribute to the overall message and impact. This will help you see how your skills in communication design and editing can be applied in different contexts.

## Session 3 · chat

**Student**

How could I build an editing portfolio from schoolwork without sharing classmates' names?

**Mentor**

To build an editing portfolio using your yearbook skills without sharing classmates' names, you could focus on editing mock-up articles or designs for a school newsletter or a fictional student-run publication. You can create scenarios where you edit work from different students or even use your own work from previous projects. This way, you maintain privacy while still practicing your editing skills. For each piece, include your edits and explain your reasoning. This will help you build a portfolio that showcases your editing abilities effectively.
