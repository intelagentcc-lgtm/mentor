## Session 4 · explore

**Student**

Can you suggest a simple way to organize a small herb planter?

**Mentor**

Great question! For organizing a small herb planter, you can start by choosing a container that fits your space and has good drainage. A simple way to organize your herbs is to use a tiered planter or a vertical planter with multiple levels. This not only looks neat but also allows you to grow more herbs in less space. For each level, you can group herbs based on their light and water needs. For example, you might place sun-loving herbs like basil and thyme on the top level and shade-tolerant herbs like chives and mints on the bottom level. You can also use labels or markers to keep track of each herb's name and care requirements. Would you like to explore this idea further or consider other types of planters?

## Session 5 · chat

**Student**

I removed the names, but the caption still identifies the event. Is there a safer version I could put in my samples?

**Mentor**

That’s a great question! When removing names from captions, it’s important to maintain the context and impact of the event. You could consider using placeholders like 'John Doe' or 'Jane Smith' for individuals involved, and describe the event in a general way. For example, instead of 'The event was attended by John Doe and Jane Smith', you could say 'The event was attended by two individuals and featured a keynote speech'. This way, you preserve the essence of the event while protecting privacy.
