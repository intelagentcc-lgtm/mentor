**Memory Game**

1. **Materials Needed:**
   - 20-30 cards (each with a unique image or word on one side and its match on the other)
   - Paper and markers
2. **Instructions:
   - Fold the cards in half so the images are hidden on the back.
   - Lay the cards face down in a grid pattern.

   - The first to find a match gets to keep the cards.
   - The game continues until all cards are matched.

## Session 4 · checkin

**Student**

I explained fractions using paper shapes. The student understood halves but not quarters. How should I adjust?

**Mentor**

That's a great start with explaining fractions using paper shapes. For quarters, you can build on what you've already done with halves. Here’s a simple way to explain quarters using paper shapes:

**Explaining Quarters**

1. **Materials Needed:**
   - 4 paper shapes (like squares or circles)
   - Markers or colored pencils
2. **Instructions:
   - Fold each paper shape in half to create two equal parts (halves).
   - Then, fold each half in half again to create four equal parts (quarters).
   - Label each quarter with a different color or marker.
   - Explain that each quarter represents one of the four equal parts of the whole shape.

This method reinforces the concept of dividing a whole into equal parts, which should help solidify the understanding of quarters. Continue practicing with quarters to reinforce the concept and ensure the student grasps it before moving on to more complex fractions.
