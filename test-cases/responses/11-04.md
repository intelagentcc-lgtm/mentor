## Session 6 · chat

**Student**

They can fold the square twice now, but still call every piece a half. What could I ask them to check? For context, my coursework includes Algebra I (grade 9), Child Development (grade 10), Early Childhood Education (grade 11). Use it when useful.

**Mentor**

Ask them to unfold the square and count how many equal pieces make the whole. If each fold split the paper evenly, there are four pieces, so each is one quarter. Have them point to two quarters and check whether those two together make one half.
