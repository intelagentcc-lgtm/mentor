## Session 1 · explore

**Student**

How can I try sports analytics and media production in one small project?

**Mentor**

Great question! For a small project combining sports analytics and media production, you could create a video analysis of a recent sports event. Use your spreadsheet skills to gather data on key moments, and then incorporate that data into a short video. For example, you could analyze a basketball game and highlight key plays, player statistics, and game trends. This project would allow you to apply your knowledge of both subjects.

### Follow-up 1 · explore

**Student**

I also want to explore architecture. What's a beginner building-design task I could try?

**Mentor**

Great choice! Exploring architecture can be both fun and educational. For a beginner task, you could try designing a simple floor plan for a small room like a study or a small bedroom. Use your spreadsheet skills to organize the measurements and dimensions. Then, create a basic elevation view and a perspective drawing. This will help you understand the basics of architectural design and layout. You can also look up some basic architectural principles and materials to incorporate into your design. Start with a simple sketch and then refine it. This project will give you a good foundation for further exploration in architecture.

## Session 4 · explore

**Student**

What is an easy way to outline a short story in one afternoon?

**Mentor**

For a short story project, you can outline it in one afternoon by following these steps: First, choose a theme or setting that interests you. Next, create a main character and a conflict. Then, outline the beginning, middle, and end of the story. Include key events and dialogue that move the story forward. Finally, revise your outline to ensure it flows logically and captures the essence of your story. This approach should help you get a solid foundation for your short story in a day.

## Session 5 · chat

**Student**

I have the average in the spreadsheet and only 30 seconds of footage. What should go first?

**Mentor**

Great question! Given you have the average in the spreadsheet and only 30 seconds of footage, you can start by incorporating the key statistics and analysis into the video. For instance, you could show the average score difference between teams, highlight the top performers, or analyze a significant moment from the game. This will ensure that the statistics are clearly communicated within the time limit. After that, you can transition to the video footage, focusing on the most impactful moments that support your analysis. What do you think about starting with the average score difference and then showing a highlight of a key play?
