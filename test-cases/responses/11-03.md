## Session 1 · explore

**Student**

How can I try sports analytics and media production in one small project?

**Mentor**

Great question! For a small project combining sports analytics and media production, you could create a video analysis of a recent sports event. Use your video editing skills to highlight key moments, and incorporate analytics like player performance data or statistics. This project will allow you to apply both your interests and skills. You could start by choosing a recent sports event, gathering data from reliable sources, and then editing a short video to present your findings. This project not only aligns with your career interests but also allows you to explore both areas in a practical way.

## Session 3 · plan

**Student**

Help me split a sports statistics video into two tasks I can complete in one week.

**Mentor**

Plan:
1. Break the video analysis into two parts: one focusing on player performance data and the other on key moments highlighted in the event.
2. Assign each part to a specific day of the week to ensure you have a clear timeline.
3. For the player performance data, gather and analyze recent statistics from a sports league or team. For key moments, watch and note down the most significant plays or sequences from the event.
Short goal: Split the video analysis into two tasks for one week.
