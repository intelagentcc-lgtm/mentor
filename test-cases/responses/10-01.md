## Session 5 · explore

**Student**

I'd like to try writing a short mystery. What's a beginner outline I could sketch?

**Mentor**

Great choice on trying a short mystery! For a beginner outline, you can start with the following steps. First, decide on a setting and a main character. Then, outline the main conflict and the resolution. Here’s a simple structure you can use as a starting point: [S1]

1. **Introduction** - Introduce the setting and the main character. Set up the initial situation or problem.
2. **Rising Action** - Develop the conflict. Add obstacles and complications that make the situation more complex.
3. **Climax** - Reach the peak of the story where the conflict is most intense.
4. **Falling Action** - Resolve the main conflict. Show how the problem is solved or how the character overcomes the challenge.
5. **Resolution** - Wrap up the story. Show the aftermath and how the character has changed.

For your Python skills, you could also consider writing a simple program that could be used in your mystery story, like a character database or a clue tracker. Would you like to explore this further or move on to the next step in your mystery writing?
