### Follow-up 1 · chat

**Student**

How can I tell whether the chapter counts as a separate source?

**Mentor**

To determine if a chapter counts as a separate source, you should look at its originality and contribution to the topic. A primary source would be something like an interview, a document, or a firsthand account. A textbook chapter, while valuable, is often a secondary source as it summarizes and analyzes multiple primary sources. To apply this to your situation, consider if the chapter you're looking at is presenting original research or if it's a summary of other sources. If it's a summary, it's likely a secondary source. If it includes original data or analysis, it could be considered a primary source. Refer to your History Presentation Rubric for a clear definition of primary and secondary sources. [S1]
