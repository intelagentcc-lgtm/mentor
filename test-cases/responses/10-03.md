## Session 2 · example

**Student**

Show a worked example of scaling a 4 by 6 photo to a proportional print with a width of 8.

**Mentor**

Let's scale your 4 by 6 inch photo to a proportional print with a width of 8 inches. First, we need to determine the scaling factor. The original width is 4 inches, and the new width is 8 inches. The scaling factor is calculated as follows:

Scaling Factor = New Width / Original Width = 8 / 4 = 2

Now, we apply this scaling factor to the original height to maintain the aspect ratio. The original height is 6 inches. So, the new height is calculated as:

New Height = Original Height * Scaling Factor = 6 * 2 = 12 inches

Therefore, the scaled dimensions of your photo will be 8 inches by 12 inches. This ensures the photo looks proportional and maintains its original aspect ratio. You can use this method for any similar scaling tasks in your photography projects.
