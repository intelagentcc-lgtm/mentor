### Follow-up 1 · chat

**Student**

How can I tell if the first sentence is doing too much?

**Mentor**

The question you asked is about identifying if a sentence is doing too much work. In writing, sometimes a sentence can be too complex or contain too much information, which can make it harder to understand. For example, if a sentence is trying to convey multiple ideas or details in one statement, it might be doing too much. In your case, you can try to break down the sentence into smaller, more manageable parts. For instance, if you're writing a paragraph about your experience with drawing, you could start with a simple sentence about what you drew, then add more details in subsequent sentences. This can help keep your writing clear and focused.
