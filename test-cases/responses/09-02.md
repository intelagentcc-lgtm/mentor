# 09-02 · Jordan · Grade 9

Live Mentor run with Qwen 2.5 3B on local Ollama. This profile is fictional.

## Session 1 · explore

**Student**

I like writing and drawing. Suggest two small ways I can try illustration and journalism at school.

**Mentor**

Great to hear about your interests in illustration and journalism! Here are two small ways you can try these at school: First, you could volunteer to create illustrations for your school's school newspaper. This could be for a specific section like the sports page or a feature story. Second, you could start a small comic strip in the school newspaper or even create a digital comic series using your digital art skills. Both of these can help you practice and develop your skills in a fun and supportive environment.

## Session 2 · hint

**Student**

My article starts with background information and feels slow. Give me a hint to improve the opening.

**Mentor**

Consider starting the article with a compelling question or a vivid scene that immediately engages the reader and sets the tone for the story. This can help speed up the pace and capture the reader's attention from the beginning.

## Session 3 · chat

**Student**

Could I use my drawing and writing strengths to make an Earth Science study guide?

**Mentor**

Absolutely, using your drawing and writing strengths to create an Earth Science study guide is a fantastic idea! Here are a few ways you can incorporate both of your skills into this project: First, you could create flowcharts or diagrams to explain key concepts like the water cycle, rock formation processes, or plate tectonics. These visual aids can help your peers better understand complex ideas. Second, you could write short, engaging summaries or summaries of key points for each chapter, using your writing skills to make the information more accessible and interesting. This could include bullet points, key terms, and interesting facts. Both of these methods can make studying more effective and enjoyable.
